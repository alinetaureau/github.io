>>>>>>>> ME & AI

Made to exist for real.

AI is one tool among others. The eye, the hand and the print know-how stay mine.

01Sketch first

Every project starts by hand. Composition, character, pose, type: everything is decided on paper before I open any software.

02AI, if you want it

I use it to explore render options when the project calls for it and you agree. Don't want it? I won't touch it. We decide together at the brief.

03Digital painting: 100% by hand

Every digital painting in this gallery is painted by hand, stroke by stroke. No AI in their render.

04Nothing leaves as is

When AI comes into a project, I rework everything: composition, hands, light, texture, type. The final image goes through my eye and my hand, from the first pixel to the last.

>>>>>>>> 05 · AI AND ME

AI, yes. Reality first.

AI helps me search, never finish. What I make ends up on paper, fabric, objects: a poster, a tee, a cover. Years in print production taught me what a screen won't show: blacks that fill in, colours that shift, prints that survive the wash. That can't be faked.

A composition eye that keeps learning. An artist's soul. A print trade. All three together.
